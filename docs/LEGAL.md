# Legal & Compliance Framework

## Legal Disclaimer

**IMPORTANT: THIS SOFTWARE IS PROVIDED FOR EDUCATIONAL AND INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY. IT DOES NOT CONSTITUTE LEGAL ADVICE.**

### No Attorney-Client Relationship

The use of Legal Contract Analyzer does not create an attorney-client relationship. The author, Nwaeke Calixtus Ifeanyi, Esq., is a qualified legal practitioner in Nigeria, but:

- This tool provides automated analysis based on keyword patterns and rule-based algorithms
- The analysis is not a substitute for professional legal judgment

### Jurisdictional Limitations

This tool is primarily trained on English-language common law contract patterns. It may not accurately identify clauses in:

- Civil law jurisdictions
- Contracts not written in English
- Highly specialized industry contracts
- Jurisdictions with unique legal requirements

Users in jurisdictions outside Nigeria should consult local counsel.

### Regulatory Compliance

#### Data Protection
- This tool processes contract text locally on the user's machine
- No contract data is transmitted to external servers
- Users are responsible for ensuring their use complies with applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, NDPR, etc.)

#### AI Transparency
- This tool uses rule-based NLP, not machine learning models
- Results are deterministic and auditable
- Users can review the source code to understand how decisions are made

### Intellectual Property

- The software is licensed under MIT License
- Users retain all rights to their contract documents
- Analysis outputs are generated for the user's own use

### Ethical AI Usage

This tool is designed to assist legal professionals, not replace them. Responsible usage includes:

1. Always having a qualified attorney review contract analysis
2. Not relying solely on automated risk scores for legal decisions
3. Understanding the limitations of keyword-based analysis
4. Using the tool to augment, not replace, legal expertise
