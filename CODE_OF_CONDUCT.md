# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1. By participating, you agree to the following standards and policies.

1. Be professional and civil. Focus on technical critique; avoid ad hominem attacks.
2. No harassment, discrimination, or hateful language of any kind.
3. Respect diverse legal traditions and jurisdictions.
4. When raising issues or PRs, include reproducible steps and redact confidential information.
5. Maintain confidentiality: do not post or attach client materials, privileged communications, or personal data.

Enforcement

- Report violations to `calixtusnwaeke@gmail.com` with evidence (links, screenshots, timestamps).
- The maintainer or designated admins will review reports and take action: warning, contribution removal, or banning.

This Code of Conduct is intentionally strict because the repository contains legal research and sensitive examples; maintain professionalism at all times.
